We have implemented an Active-Active deployment strategy for the MICS services using multiple replicas and pod anti-affinity.

As part of the validation, we are verifying that a minimum of two healthy replicas are running on different GKE nodes and that both are registered as healthy service endpoints.

We will also perform a controlled pod failover test in QA by terminating one replica and verifying that the application remains accessible through the surviving replica while Kubernetes automatically provisions a replacement.

This strategy is intended to minimize service disruptions caused by individual pod or node failures and improve application availability across environments.
